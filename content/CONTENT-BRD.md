# Ngagdra Dorji Choegar — Content BRD

## North star
Ngagdra Dorji Choegar's website exists to document, explain and communicate a living institution—its teaching, practice, people, lineage, places and public life. Fundraising supports that mission; it is not the mission of the website.

## Journey
Understand → Appreciate → Trust → Explore → Participate → Support

## Page formula
1. Meaning — why should I care?
2. Explanation — what is it?
3. Evidence — what actually happens?
4. Human story — who does it affect?
5. Detail — deeper history/context.
6. Next action — where can I go from here?

## Editorial taxonomy
IDENTITY · LINEAGE · EDUCATION · PRACTICE · PRESERVATION · PEOPLE · PLACES · PUBLIC LIFE · STORIES · SUPPORT

## Writing rules
- Headline for meaning, not bureaucracy.
- Opening paragraphs: 40–60 words before a new visual hierarchy.
- Web paragraphs: usually 2–4 sentences.
- Keep authentic Buddhist terminology, explain it on first use.
- Level A: everybody. Level B: interested visitor. Level C: practitioner/scholar.
- Never make unsupported promises about public access, accommodation, events or donation use.
