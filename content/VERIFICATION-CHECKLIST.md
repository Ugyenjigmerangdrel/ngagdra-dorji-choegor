# Pre-launch verification checklist

The site intentionally leaves uncertain facts out of structured data and operational claims.

## Verify before launch
- [ ] Current monk/student count (the publication records nearly 200 at its time of writing; the public site does not present this as current).
- [ ] Current number of retreat centres supported (the publication and brief use different summary figures; the public directory includes all 18 documented institutions and sites).
- [ ] Official institutional email, phone and social profiles.
- [ ] Exact public-facing address and map coordinates.
- [ ] Visitor access rules and opening/appointment requirements.
- [ ] Whether visitor accommodation exists and under what conditions.
- [ ] All future event dates, locations, teachers and attendance rules.
- [ ] Official donation/payment route, legal entity name and bank/payment information.
- [ ] Institute-approved biography of H.E. Gyeltshen Trulku Rinpoche.
- [ ] Final spelling/transliteration conventions for names and Buddhist terms.
- [ ] Dzongkha translations and script line-height review.
- [ ] Current network centre names, types, locations, management and active programmes.
- [ ] Rights/credits for all photography and archival material.
- [ ] Final official emblem/vector assets and favicon variant.

## Source-grounded facts currently used
- Founded: 26 April 2007.
- Major consecration / Hey Vajra statue: 30 October 2015.
- Location: Kurizampa, Mongar, Eastern Bhutan; above the Kurichu River / Kurizampa bridge.
- Purpose: advanced Vajrayana education and preparation before longer retreat.
- Reported facilities: classrooms, Dharma Assembly Hall, dormitories, library.
- Reported learning areas: Vajrayana, mantra, sutra, sciences, astrology, mandala studies, English.
